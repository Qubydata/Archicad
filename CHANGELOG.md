# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- AI Assistant: chat with Claude to change your Archicad model, in the Tevavid panel inside Archicad or from Claude Desktop with your Claude plan.
- Sun Analysis: live daylight, direct-sun hours and daylight factor on your rooms in the 3D window, with a time slider. Uses the project location, glass and standard reflections.
- Setup wizard: pick your Archicad versions and tools; installs the Tapir add-on and the panel.
- Automatic updates, and running a newer setup updates the installed copy and keeps your settings.
