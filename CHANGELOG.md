# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-09)

- The Tevavid menu in Archicad's menu bar: AI Assistant, Sun Analysis, Site from Map, Model Import, Settings. Our own Archicad add-on replaces Tapir, so no uv or Python scripts are needed.
- Site from Map: the real terrain and neighbouring buildings around the project location, or a surveyor's points and contours, as an Archicad Mesh.
- Model Import: bring in FBX, OBJ, glTF, 3DS and other 3D models (for example from D5 Works), remove parts, reduce polygons with a slider and a green/amber/red budget, and save them as objects in your library folders (Furniture > Tables...).
- Sun Analysis works on Archicad versions that reject element filters.

## 0.1.4 (2026-11-09)

- Sun Analysis works on Archicad versions that reject element filters (error 4002 on '#/filters').

## 0.1.3 (2026-11-09)

- Tapir finds uv after installing: no more 'Tapir could not find uv'.

## 0.1.2 (2026-11-09)

- Setup on Windows installs again (0.1.1 stopped with 'Main window failed to start').
- The starting picture no longer lists files while setup unpacks.

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-03)

- Setup opens on Windows (it closed straight away in 0.1.0).
- A starting picture while setup unpacks, and a clear message if setup cannot open.
- Uninstall removes the app folder on Windows.
- New Tevavid logo.

## 0.1.0

- AI Assistant: chat with Claude to change your Archicad model, in the Tevavid panel inside Archicad or from Claude Desktop with your Claude plan.
- Sun Analysis: live daylight, direct-sun hours and daylight factor on your rooms in the 3D window, with a time slider. Uses the project location, glass and standard reflections.
- Setup wizard: pick your Archicad versions and tools; installs the Tapir add-on and the panel.
- Automatic updates, and running a newer setup updates the installed copy and keeps your settings.
